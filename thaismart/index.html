Thai Smart: Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Thai Smart ("the app") is an app for learning to speak and read Thai, published by ZoolVibes on Google Play. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version

Your progress and settings stay on your phone. We don't have servers, there are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we never collect, share or sell your data.

Your progress and settings

The words you've learned, quiz scores, practice time, saved phrases and settings are stored only on your phone. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app data in your own device backup, which is handled by Google under your account settings. We cannot see it.

Microphone and your voice

The microphone is used only when you choose to practise speaking. In Listen & repeat, the app records your voice so you can play it back and compare it with the Thai voice. That recording is kept in the app's temporary storage on your phone, replaced by your next attempt, and never sent to us.

When you tap a "Speak", "Say it" or "Check me" button, the app asks your phone's speech recognition service (usually Google's) to turn what you say into text. That service may process your speech on Google's servers, under Google's Privacy Policy. The app only receives the text and does not keep it.

Translation and the Thai voice

Free talk uses Google's ML Kit translator, which runs on your phone. The language model is downloaded once from Google, and after that your sentences are translated on the device without being sent anywhere. Thai speech is spoken by your phone's text-to-speech engine. Google Play services and the text-to-speech engine may send technical information (such as device and app details, and usage and error data about these features) to Google, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Thai Smart Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether you have bought Pro on your account. The Google Play billing component in the app may send technical information about the purchase process to Google, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us.

Permissions

Microphone (only when you practise speaking), notifications (only if you turn on the reminder), and permission to restart the reminder after your phone restarts. The app does not access your location, contacts, photos or files.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting of its own and no third-party tracking.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Reset all progress in the app, or uninstall the app or clear its storage, to remove everything it stored. Nothing is stored on our side, so there's nothing for us to delete.

Learning content

Phrases, romanization and travel information are a learning aid. Have a Thai speaker check them before you rely on them, and check official sources for entry rules and other travel requirements.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
