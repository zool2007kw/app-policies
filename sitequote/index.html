SiteQuote: Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026

SiteQuote ("the app") is a quoting and site-visit app for tradespeople and small building firms, published by zool on Google Play. This policy explains how the app handles your information and your clients' information.

The short version

Everything you put in the app stays on your phone. We don't have servers, we can't see your jobs, clients, photos or quotes, and we never collect, share or sell any data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What the app stores, and where

Your business details and logo, jobs, client names and contact details, site addresses, notes, measurements, checklists, quotes and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, and it is never uploaded by the app.

Client details

You type in your clients' details yourself, or pick a client with "From contacts". That opens your phone's own contact picker, and the app receives only the name and phone number of the one contact you choose. The app does not have permission to read your contacts and cannot see any others.

Photos

When you take a photo, the app opens your phone's camera app and receives only the photo you take. When you choose one, Android's photo picker gives the app only the photos you select; the app cannot see the rest of your gallery. The app keeps a resized copy in its private storage and does not add photos to your gallery.

Quotes and reports you share

The app creates quote and site report PDFs on your phone. They only leave your phone when you choose to share them, using Android's share menu (for example email or WhatsApp). Where they go and who can see them is up to you and the app you share with.

Backups

If you use Backup, the app writes a file containing your data and photos to a location you choose with Android's file picker, for example your Downloads folder or Google Drive. Where that file goes is up to you and the storage service you pick. Restore reads only the file you select.

Ratings

The app may show Google Play's in-app review prompt once. Any review you leave is handled by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy; the app is not told whether you left one.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. It does not access your location, contacts list, microphone, or any photos other than the ones you take or pick. Call, Text, Email and Directions buttons simply open your phone's own apps with the client's details filled in.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking.

Your clients' information

If you store your clients' details in the app, you are responsible for handling them in line with the data protection rules that apply to your business (for example GDPR). Deleting a job deletes its client details and photos from the app.

Deleting your data

Delete a job to remove it and its photos. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes everything. Backup files and PDFs you saved or shared yourself are yours to delete. Nothing is stored on our side, so there's nothing for us to delete.

Children

The app is a business tool, is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
