Survival101: Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Survival101 ("the app") is an offline emergency and survival guide, published by ZoolVibes on Google Play. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version

Everything you enter stays on your phone. The app works fully offline and has no internet access of its own. We don't have servers, there are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we never collect, share or sell your data.

What the app stores on your phone

Your settings, saved guides, the guides you have marked as read, your checklist ticks and, if you use it, the "My emergency plan" card (contacts, meeting places, medical notes and other notes you type) are stored only on your phone. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app data in your own device backup, which is handled by Google under your account settings. We cannot see it.

The emergency call button

The call button opens your phone app with your emergency number filled in. You press call yourself. To choose the right number automatically, the app reads your mobile network's or SIM's country code from your phone; it does not use your location and does not send this anywhere.

Purchases

Survival101 Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether you have bought Pro on your account. The Google Play billing component in the app may send technical information about the purchase process to Google, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Ratings

The app may show Google Play's own rating window once. Google Play handles it; the app is not told what you choose.

Permissions

The app asks for no runtime permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting of its own and no third-party tracking.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove everything it stored. You can also clear a checklist's ticks, or delete the text in "My emergency plan", at any time. Nothing is stored on our side, so there's nothing for us to delete.

Guide content

The guides give general information based on official public advice. They are not medical advice and do not replace first aid training. Always follow instructions from the emergency services and local authorities.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
