Full Stop: Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Full Stop ("the app") is a period tracker published by zool on Google Play. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version

Everything you record in Full Stop stays on your phone. We don't have servers, we can't see your data, and we never collect, share or sell it. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your phone only:

  • Periods you log: start and end dates, flow, symptoms and notes
  • Daily check-ins: mood, symptoms and optional notes
  • Your settings: the name you'd like to be called, your goal (tracking, trying to conceive or avoiding pregnancy), default cycle and period lengths, reminder time, colour theme and app lock

Predictions, reminders, the home-screen widget and "Your patterns" are all worked out on your phone. None of this information is sent to us or to anyone else.

Backups and exports you choose

If your phone backs up apps to your Google account (Android backup), your Full Stop data is included in that encrypted backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This is controlled by your phone's backup settings and handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

You can also save a backup file or share it (for example by email or to cloud storage). This only happens when you tap the button, and the file goes wherever you choose to send it.

Purchases (Full Stop Plus)

Full Stop Plus is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether Plus has been bought on your account. The Google Play billing component built into the app may send technical information about the purchase process to Google, under Google's Privacy Policy. Your periods, check-ins and notes are never part of this.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking libraries. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. It asks for permission to show notifications (only if you turn on reminders) and uses your phone's fingerprint, face or PIN unlock if you turn on app lock; the app never sees your fingerprint or PIN.

Deleting your data

You can delete any period or check-in in the app. To remove everything, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app. Because nothing is stored on our side, there's nothing for us to delete.

Not medical advice

Predictions are estimates based on what you log. Full Stop is not a medical device and must not be used as contraception.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
