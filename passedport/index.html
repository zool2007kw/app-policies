Passedport: Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Passedport ("the app") is a passport and ID photo maker published by zool on Google Play. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version

Your photos are processed on your phone and never uploaded to us. We don't have servers, we can't see your photos, and we never collect, share or sell your data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Your photos

When you take a photo, the app opens your phone's own camera app and receives only the photo you take. When you choose a photo, Android's photo picker gives the app only the photo you select; the app cannot see the rest of your gallery.

Face finding, cropping, the photo checks and background replacement all happen on your phone. The photo is kept in the app's memory while you edit it and is discarded when you leave it. The app does not keep a copy.

When you save, the finished print sheet and photo are written to your phone's Pictures/Passedport folder. When you tap Share, they go only to the app or person you choose.

Google Play services

Face finding and background replacement use Google's ML Kit. Some of its on-device models are downloaded and updated by Google Play services. Your photos are processed on the device and are not sent to Google by ML Kit. Google Play services may send technical information (such as device and app details, and usage and error data about these features) to Google, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The lifetime unlock and photo passes are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play what you have bought on your account. The Google Play billing component in the app may send technical information about the purchase process to Google, under Google's Privacy Policy. Your photos are never part of this.

Permissions

On Android 10 and newer the app asks for no permissions. On Android 8 and 9 it asks for storage permission only when you save, so it can write the photos to your Pictures folder. The app does not access your location, contacts, microphone, or your photos other than the one you pick or take.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking.

Deleting your data

Photos you saved are in your phone's Pictures/Passedport folder and can be deleted from your gallery. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the rest (for example, unused photo passes). Nothing is stored on our side, so there's nothing for us to delete.

Acceptance of your photo

The app sizes photos to published official requirements, but the issuing authority decides whether a photo is accepted. Always check the official rules for your document.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
