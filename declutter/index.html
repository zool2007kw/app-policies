Declutter My Home: Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Declutter My Home ("the app") is a room-by-room decluttering planner published by zool on Google Play. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version

Everything you put in the app stays on your phone. We don't have servers, we can't see your rooms, lists or photos, and we never collect, share or sell your data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What the app stores, and where

Your rooms, checklists, out-the-door and maybe-box lists, memories, sessions, challenge progress and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, and it is never uploaded by the app.

Your photos

Before-and-after photos and memory photos are optional. When you take one, the app opens your phone's own camera app and receives only the photo you take. When you choose one, Android's photo picker gives the app only the photo you select; the app cannot see the rest of your gallery. The app keeps a resized copy in its private storage. It does not add photos to your gallery.

Backups

If you use Backup (a Pro feature), the app writes a file containing your data and photos to a location you choose with Android's file picker, for example your Downloads folder or Google Drive. Where that file goes and who can see it is up to you and the storage service you pick. Restore reads only the file you select.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules one reminder a day on your phone. The reminder text is created on your phone from your own data.

Purchases

Declutter My Home Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether you have bought Pro on your account. The Google Play billing component in the app may send technical information about the purchase process to Google, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Ratings

The app may show Google Play's in-app review prompt once. Any review you leave is handled by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy; the app is not told whether you left one.

Permissions

Notifications (only if you turn reminders on), vibration (to signal the end of a timed session) and "run at startup" (so your reminder survives a restart). The app does not access your location, contacts, microphone, or any photos other than the ones you take or pick.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes everything in the app, photos included. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage does the same. Backup files you saved yourself are yours to delete. Nothing is stored on our side, so there's nothing for us to delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
