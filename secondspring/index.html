Second Spring: Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Second Spring ("the app") is a menopause and perimenopause symptom tracker published by zool on Google Play. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version

Second Spring does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal data. Everything you log stays on your phone. The app has no accounts and no servers, and it does not send your information anywhere.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves what you enter locally on your phone: daily symptom logs and severity, hot flash and night sweat counts, sleep hours and quality, energy and day ratings, notes, period start dates, journal entries and tags, and your settings (theme and reminder choices). This information never leaves your device unless you choose to export it, and it is not accessible to us.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app. Individual period and journal entries can also be removed inside the app.

Exports and backups you create

The Doctor Visit tab lets you create a summary, a spreadsheet (CSV) or a backup file (JSON). These files are only created when you tap an export button, and they are handed to your phone's share sheet so you can choose where they go (for example email or a cloud drive). Whatever you choose then handles the file under its own privacy terms. Restoring a backup only reads the file you pick.

If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your Google backup. That is controlled by you in your phone's settings and handled by Google.

Reminders

If you switch on reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone. No reminder data is sent anywhere.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking libraries. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. Fonts and articles are built into the app, so it does not load anything from the internet.

Not medical advice

Second Spring is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device. Estimates and articles are general information only. Please talk to a doctor or other health professional about your symptoms.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Google Play

When you download the app, Google Play may process information under Google's own privacy policy. That processing is handled by Google, not by this app.
